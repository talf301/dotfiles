## Review scope (required)

Judge the change only against:
- the task's acceptance criteria
- the parent specification's sections on preserving existing behavior and on non-goals, when the specification has them

Do not request extra features, guards, fallbacks or refactors beyond these. Put any such ideas under a heading "Optional (not blocking)". They must not change your verdict. A change that adds scope the task did not ask for is itself a finding that blocks approval.
